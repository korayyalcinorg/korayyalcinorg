# Koray Yalçın — Research Library & Publications Index

This directory is the human-readable research catalog for **Koray Yalçın** and complements the main publication hub at [korayyalcin.org](https://www.korayyalcin.org/yayinlar-arastirmalar/).

The library is organized around real estate CRM, lead management, RevOps, marketing automation, PropTech, AdTech and AI/GEO visibility.

> Canonical source for full articles and publication pages: **https://www.korayyalcin.org**

## Books & Monographs

### Gayrimenkul Sektöründe Lead Yönetimi ve Müşteri Dönüşüm Sistemleri

A practical reference on real-estate lead generation, CRM architecture, lead routing, lead scoring, WhatsApp/e-mail automation, sales pipelines, dashboards and AI-assisted growth systems.

- Type: Book / sector reference
- Year: 2026
- Publication hub: https://www.korayyalcin.org/kitaplar/

### Kuzey Kıbrıs Gayrimenkul Sektöründe Yapay Zeka Destekli Revenue Operations (RevOps) Sistemleri

Academic monograph focused on North Cyprus real-estate funnels, multi-currency sales pipelines, multilingual communication flows, CRM, marketing automation and AI-assisted RevOps.

- Type: Academic monograph
- Year: 2026
- Publication hub: https://www.korayyalcin.org/kitaplar/

## Benchmark Research

### Gayrimenkul Müşteri Adayı (Lead) Dönüşüm ve Yanıt Süresi Benchmark Raporu 2026

Aggregated benchmark study covering **24,850 digital leads across 18 residential projects over a 12-month review period**.

Focus areas: Speed-to-Lead, channel CPL, appointment conversion, sales conversion and lead-to-sale operations.

- Article: https://www.korayyalcin.org/yayinlar-arastirmalar/gayrimenkul-lead-donusum-ve-yanit-suresi-benchmark-2026/
